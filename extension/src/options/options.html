Autofill profile

Stored only in this browser (chrome.storage.local). Nothing is uploaded.

Your resumes

Add your resume PDF and the profile is filled in from it: name, contact details, links, jobs, schools and skills (work eligibility and saved answers aren't on a resume, so they're kept). Add an updated resume any time: the newest one is used from then on, and the profile refreshes from it. To send a different resume for certain jobs, give it words to look for in the job title, such as devsecops, security. Changes are highlighted, and nothing is kept until you click Save.

Saved answers

Answers to questions forms ask often. When filling, the extension matches a form's question to the closest one here; unmatched questions are left blank for you.

Apply queue limits

The job site's Apply to saved with Autofill skips jobs past these limits, so you don't flood one company or apply to more than you can follow up on. 0 means no limit.

Draft answers with Claude (optional)

With your own Anthropic API key, Draft open questions with Claude in the popup writes first drafts for questions like "Why do you want to work here?", for you to edit. Sent to Anthropic: the job's title, company and description, the questions, and your work history, education, skills and saved answers. Never sent: your name, email, phone, address or links. Each draft uses a few cents of API credit on your account. Get a key at console.anthropic.com.

Back up and restore

Your applications live only in this browser; removing the extension deletes them. Turning this on asks for permission to save downloads. The backup restores on the My applications page.

Import takes a file exported here, or a "Back up" file from the job site (its apply kit fills the basics).
