Experience

Apply quickly

  1. ☆ Save the postings you want (or press s).
  2. Download saved for prep, then in Terminal:
    .venv/bin/python apply/jobprep.py batch ~/Downloads/prep-batch-….json
    Each gets a memo PDF, an email, and answers.txt with "why this company" and a cover note. It asks before using the API.
  3. Press Apply. The employer's form opens in a new tab and your apply kit appears here with Copy buttons. Fill in the form, attach the memo, submit it yourself, then Mark as applied.
  4. Follow-ups come due 6 days later under My applications.

        About the data

        How current it is

        Survey estimates from May 2025, published in 2026. Not live job-market data.

        Concentration is not opportunity

        The location quotient (LQ) compares a job's share of local employment with its share nationally. An LQ of 1.0 is average and 3.0 means three times as common as usual. A high LQ means the job is unusually concentrated in a place. It does not mean employers there are hiring, or that moving there is a good idea. Check job postings, cost of living and wages before deciding anything.

        By default, metros with fewer than 50 jobs in an occupation are left out of LQ rankings, so a handful of jobs in a small place can't top the list. "Number of jobs" answers a different question: where the most people do this work.

        Missing is not zero

        BLS withholds estimates that are unreliable or could identify an employer. Those metros show as no data: a flat grey dot on the globe, a dashed ring on the map, and a separate list under the table. They never count as low values. Where BLS published jobs but not an LQ, we calculate one from its employment totals and mark it with †.

        Your resume

        Resume files are read with your browser's file API and processed by code served from this site. No network request carries resume text, and the page's Content Security Policy only allows connections to this site's own static files. Matching is keyword-based and often wrong. Treat the suggestions as a starting point.

        Coverage

        US metropolitan statistical areas only. Rural and nonmetro areas, and all countries outside the US, are not included yet.

        Job postings

        The postings tab lists live openings that mention the programming languages in jobs/sources.json (Rust, C++, Go, Python and others). They come from the public job-board APIs of the companies in jobs/sources.json (Greenhouse, Lever, Ashby), the latest Hacker News "Who is hiring?" thread and, if you add API keys, Adzuna and USAJobs. A posting can appear because it lists a language once among several; "Focused postings only" hides those. Postings that only say "Remote" or name a city outside the US are in the list but not on the map. Hybrid comes from the location field or phrases like "3 days a week in the office"; On-site means a posting names a place and mentions neither, so some of those are really hybrid. Postings refresh automatically: closed roles drop out of company boards, and feed postings older than 60 days are removed. Applying always happens on the employer's own site.

        Applying

        Nothing on this site applies for you. Save postings, prepare tailored materials with apply/jobprep.py, then fill in and submit each application yourself on the employer's site. The apply kit only puts your own saved answers on the clipboard. Your saved postings, statuses and apply kit stay in this browser's storage and are never sent anywhere.
